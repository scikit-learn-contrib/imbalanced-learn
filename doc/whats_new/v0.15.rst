- Add :class:`~imblearn.over_sampling.MLSMOTE`, an over-sampler for
  imbalanced multilabel data based on Charte et al. (2015). It detects
  minority labels with the imbalance ratio per label and generates synthetic
  samples by interpolating continuous features and voting categorical
  features and labelsets among nearest neighbors.
  :pr:`1213` by :user:`OmarAlaaeldein <OmarAlaaeldein>`.
