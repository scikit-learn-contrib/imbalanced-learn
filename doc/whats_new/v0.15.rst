Bug fixes
.........

- Fix :class:`~imblearn.over_sampling.ADASYN` raising a ``ValueError`` when the
  requested number of samples is small: the per-neighbor number of samples to
  generate was rounded independently and could be zero for all neighbors. The
  samples are now allocated proportionally so that the requested number of
  samples is always generated.
  :issue:`1245` by :user:`Davis <davrens101>`.
